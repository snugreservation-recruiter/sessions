About SessionActivity

SessionActivity was born from a simple need: peace of mind. Whether you're a parent, a solo traveler, a field worker, or caring for an elderly loved one — knowing where a device is, at the right moment, shouldn't require constant checking.

🎯 Our Mission

Give people a simple, private, and reliable way to receive location updates about their own devices — delivered to their inbox and phone on their terms.
