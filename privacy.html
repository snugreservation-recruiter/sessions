Privacy Policy

SessionActivity tracks only your own device, only after you grant permission. We never track third parties and we never sell your data.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, and phone number
  • Your device's GPS coordinates when a ping fires
  • Timestamps of each ping

Your rights

You can delete your data at any time from the app, or by emailing support@sessionactivity.app.
